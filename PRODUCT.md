# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Business owners in the UK, Ireland, Cyprus and beyond who want to understand why their business is not being surfaced by search engines and AI recommendation tools, then commission one specialist to fix it.

## Product Purpose

Sonar Studio explains the shift from conventional search to AI recommendations, proves the method with one honest case study, and converts qualified visitors into free teardown requests.

## Brand Personality

Precise, direct, quietly technical.

## Anti-references

Generic AI landing pages, orbit graphics, floating-dot networks, purple gradients, glassmorphism, equal feature-card grids, invented social proof, agency theatre and hype-led copy.

## Design Principles

1. Make the signal metaphor structural, not decorative.
2. Let the one real result carry the proof.
3. Keep the path to a free teardown obvious without repeating one layout.
4. Show technical confidence through speed, accessibility and clean information architecture.
5. Keep motion explanatory and interruptible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA, preserve full keyboard operation, maintain visible focus, provide direct form errors, and replace all ambient WebGL and 3D motion on mobile, reduced-motion and data-saver devices.
